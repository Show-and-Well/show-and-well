Life coaches in Minneapolis–St. Paul

A life coach helps you get clear on what you want, set goals and follow through. Coaching is focused on the present and the future, and on action.

See life coaches on Show & WellNot sure? Take the 4-question quiz

What a life coach can help with

  • Setting and reaching personal goals
  • Building confidence and habits
  • Making a big decision or life change
  • Staying accountable

What to expect

Expect a first conversation about where you are and where you want to be, then regular sessions with action steps in between. Most coaching happens by video or phone.

Credentials to look for

Coaching is not licensed in Minnesota. Many coaches hold credentials from the International Coaching Federation (ACC, PCC or MCC). Coaching is not therapy and is not a treatment for mental health conditions.

Questions to ask before you book

  • What training or credential do you have?
  • What kinds of clients and goals do you usually work with?
  • How do you measure progress?
  • How long is a typical coaching engagement?

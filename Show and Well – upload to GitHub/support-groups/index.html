Support group facilitators in Minneapolis–St. Paul

A support group brings together people facing a similar experience, such as grief, a health condition, caregiving or a life change. A facilitator keeps the group safe, structured and on track.

If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or call 911.

What a support group facilitator can help with

  • Feeling less alone
  • Learning from people who have been there
  • Grief, caregiving and life changes
  • Ongoing encouragement

What to expect

Groups usually meet weekly or monthly, in person or online. You can often listen at first without sharing. Ask about group rules on privacy.

Credentials to look for

Facilitators come from many backgrounds. Some are licensed mental health professionals, others are trained peers. Peer-led groups do not replace treatment; a licensed professional should lead groups that are part of mental health care.

Questions to ask before you book

  • Who leads the group, and what is their training?
  • How large is the group and how often does it meet?
  • What are the rules about privacy?

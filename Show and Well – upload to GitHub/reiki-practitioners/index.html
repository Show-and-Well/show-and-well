Reiki practitioners in Minneapolis–St. Paul

Reiki is a Japanese energy practice in which the practitioner places their hands lightly on or just above your body. Many people find sessions calming and relaxing. Reiki is a complementary practice and is not a medical treatment.

What a reiki practitioner can help with

  • Relaxation
  • A quiet, restful break
  • Complementing other self-care practices

What to expect

You stay fully clothed, usually lying on a table. Sessions are quiet and often last 45 to 90 minutes. Let the practitioner know if you prefer hands-off.

Credentials to look for

Reiki is not licensed in Minnesota. Practitioners are trained in levels (Level I, II and Master or Teacher). As unlicensed practitioners, they must give you a written client bill of rights under Minnesota law.

Questions to ask before you book

  • What level of training have you completed?
  • Is the session hands-on or hands-off?
  • What should I expect during and after a session?

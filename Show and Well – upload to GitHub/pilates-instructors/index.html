Pilates instructors in Minneapolis–St. Paul

A Pilates instructor teaches controlled, low-impact exercises that build core strength, stability and posture. Sessions are done on a mat or on equipment such as the reformer.

What a Pilates instructor can help with

  • Core strength and stability
  • Posture and alignment
  • Low-impact strength training
  • Body control and balance

What to expect

Expect the instructor to watch how you move, explain the basic principles, and start with foundational exercises. Equipment sessions take place in a studio; mat sessions can also be done at home or online.

Credentials to look for

Pilates instruction is not licensed in Minnesota. Look for a comprehensive certification (one that covers mat and equipment) from an established program, or the National Pilates Certification Program (NCPT) credential.

Questions to ask before you book

  • Do you teach mat, reformer or both?
  • How long was your certification training?
  • Can you adapt sessions for back pain or pregnancy?

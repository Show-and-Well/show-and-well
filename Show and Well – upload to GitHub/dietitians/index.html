Dietitians & nutrition coaches in Minneapolis–St. Paul

Dietitians and nutrition coaches help you change how you eat. Licensed dietitians can give medical nutrition advice, for conditions such as diabetes or high cholesterol. Nutrition coaches focus on habits, meal planning and accountability.

What a dietitian or nutrition coach can help with

  • Meal planning and healthier everyday eating
  • Eating for a health condition (licensed dietitians)
  • Sports nutrition
  • Building sustainable habits without strict diets

What to expect

Expect questions about your current eating, health history, schedule and goals. Many dietitians and coaches work online, which makes it easy to fit sessions into your week.

Credentials to look for

In Minnesota, the titles "licensed dietitian" (LD) and "licensed nutritionist" (LN) are protected and granted by the Minnesota Board of Dietetics and Nutrition Practice. Many dietitians are also nationally registered (RD or RDN). If you have a medical condition, look for a licensed dietitian.

Questions to ask before you book

  • Are you licensed in Minnesota (LD or LN)?
  • Do you have experience with my goal or condition?
  • Do you work with insurance?
  • What does follow-up look like between sessions?

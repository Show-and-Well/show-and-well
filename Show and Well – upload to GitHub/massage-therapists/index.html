Massage therapists in Minneapolis–St. Paul

A massage therapist uses hands-on techniques to relax muscles, ease tension and support recovery. Styles range from gentle relaxation massage to deep tissue and sports massage.

What a massage therapist can help with

  • Muscle tension and tightness
  • Stress and relaxation
  • Recovery after training or long days on your feet
  • Comfort during pregnancy (prenatal massage)

What to expect

Your therapist should ask about your health, areas to focus on or avoid, and pressure preferences before you start. You can ask them to change pressure or stop at any time.

Credentials to look for

Minnesota does not have a statewide massage license, so rules depend on the city. St. Paul, for example, requires massage practitioners to hold a city license. Look for training from an accredited massage school, national certification such as the MBLEx or NCBTMB board certification, and liability insurance. Minnesota law also requires unlicensed health care practitioners to give clients a written bill of rights.

Questions to ask before you book

  • Where did you train, and how many hours?
  • Do you hold any national certification?
  • Do you have a city license, if your city requires one?
  • What should I tell you about my health before the session?

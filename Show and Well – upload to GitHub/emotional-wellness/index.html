Counselors, therapists and life coaches in Minneapolis–St. Paul

Emotional wellness is about understanding and handling your feelings, stress and relationships with yourself. Some people need a licensed mental health professional; others want a coach to help them set and reach goals.

Browse emotional wellness professionalsNot sure? Take the 4-question quiz

Which professional is right for you?

  • Dealing with anxiety, depression, grief or trauma? Look for a licensed counselor or therapist.
  • Feeling stuck and want structure, accountability and goals? A life coach may fit.
  • Not sure? Many therapists offer a short free consultation to talk about fit.
  • Coaches are not a substitute for mental health treatment. If you are in crisis, call or text 988.

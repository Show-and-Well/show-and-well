Strength & conditioning coaches in Minneapolis–St. Paul

A strength and conditioning coach builds structured, long-term training programs to improve strength, power, speed and endurance. Many work with athletes, but they also help everyday people who want a more performance-focused plan.

What a strength and conditioning coach can help with

  • Training for a sport or a season
  • Getting stronger with a progressive barbell or kettlebell program
  • Improving speed, power and agility
  • Building a plan that peaks for an event, like a race or a tryout

What to expect

Expect an assessment of how you move and what you can lift today, then a program broken into phases. Many coaches combine in-person sessions with a written plan you follow on your own.

Credentials to look for

Look for the Certified Strength and Conditioning Specialist (CSCS) credential from the NSCA, which requires a bachelor's degree and an exam. Coaches who focus on a specific sport may also hold that sport's coaching certifications.

Questions to ask before you book

  • What does a typical training block look like?
  • How do you adjust the plan around my schedule or an injury?
  • Do you write programs I can follow between sessions?

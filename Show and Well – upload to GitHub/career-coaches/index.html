Career coaches in Minneapolis–St. Paul

A career coach helps you figure out your next step at work and how to get there, whether that is a new job, a new field, a promotion or better balance.

See career coaches on Show & WellNot sure? Take the 4-question quiz

What a career coach can help with

  • Choosing a direction or changing careers
  • Resumes, LinkedIn and interviews
  • Negotiating pay or a promotion
  • Work-life balance and burnout

What to expect

The first session is usually about where you are and what you want. Later sessions focus on action: applications, practice interviews and plans.

Credentials to look for

Career coaching is not licensed. Many career coaches hold International Coaching Federation (ICF) credentials or career-development certifications, and experience in hiring or in your field.

Questions to ask before you book

  • What kinds of clients and industries do you usually work with?
  • What is your background?
  • What does a typical engagement look like?

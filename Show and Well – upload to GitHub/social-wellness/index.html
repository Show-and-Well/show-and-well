Relationship coaches, group fitness and support groups in the Twin Cities

Social wellness is about the connections in your life: partners, friends, family and community. Feeling connected is easier when you have the right setting, whether that is a class, a group or one-on-one help.

Browse social wellness professionalsNot sure? Take the 4-question quiz

Which professional is right for you?

  • Want to communicate better with a partner or family member? Try a relationship coach.
  • Want to exercise with other people? Group fitness classes build routine and community.
  • Going through something specific, like a loss or a life change? A support group connects you with people who understand.

Social wellness professionals

  • Relationship coaches

    A relationship coach helps individuals or couples communicate better, work through disagreements and build stronger connections.

  • Group fitness instructors

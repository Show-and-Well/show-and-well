Meditation, breathwork and reiki practitioners in Minneapolis–St. Paul

Spiritual wellness is about meaning, values and a sense of calm. It does not have to be religious. Many people start with simple practices that help them slow down and pay attention.

Which professional is right for you?

  • Want a daily practice to quiet your mind? Start with a meditation teacher.
  • Want a practical way to calm your nervous system? A breathwork coach teaches breathing techniques.
  • Curious about energy work? Reiki practitioners offer gentle, hands-on or hands-off sessions.

Spiritual wellness professionals

  • Meditation teachers

    A meditation teacher helps you build a steady practice, choose a technique that fits you and work through common challenges such as a busy mind or trouble sitting still.

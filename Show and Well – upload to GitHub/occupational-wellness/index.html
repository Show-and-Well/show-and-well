Career coaches and workplace wellness consultants in Minneapolis–St. Paul

Occupational wellness is about finding satisfaction and balance in your work. That might mean a new direction, a healthier routine at your current job, or a better culture for your team.

Which professional is right for you?

  • Thinking about a career change, a promotion or a job search? Work with a career coach.
  • Want to improve wellbeing across a team or company? A workplace wellness consultant designs programs for groups.

Occupational wellness professionals

  • Career coaches

    A career coach helps you figure out your next step at work and how to get there, whether that is a new job, a new field, a promotion or better balance.

    Guide · Browse

  • Workplace wellness consultants

    A workplace wellness consultant helps employers design programs that support employee health, such as stress management, movement breaks, ergonomics and mental health awareness.

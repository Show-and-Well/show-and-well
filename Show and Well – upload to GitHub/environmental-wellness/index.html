Ergonomics consultants, home organizers and nature therapy guides in the Twin Cities

Environmental wellness is about the spaces you live and work in, and your time outdoors. A comfortable desk, an organized home and regular time in nature can all make daily life easier.

Which professional is right for you?

  • Aches from working at a desk? An ergonomics consultant can assess your setup.
  • Overwhelmed by clutter? A professional organizer helps you sort, decide and set up systems.
  • Want to slow down outdoors? A nature or forest therapy guide leads gentle, guided walks.

Environmental wellness professionals

  • Ergonomics consultants

    An ergonomics consultant looks at how you sit, stand and work, then recommends changes to your desk, chair, screen and habits to reduce strain.

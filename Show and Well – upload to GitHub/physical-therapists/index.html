Physical therapists in Minneapolis–St. Paul

A physical therapist (PT) is a licensed health care professional who evaluates and treats problems with movement, pain and function. PTs help people recover from injuries and surgery and manage ongoing conditions.

What a physical therapist can help with

  • Recovering from an injury or surgery
  • Back, neck, knee or shoulder pain
  • Balance and fall prevention
  • Returning to a sport or activity safely

What to expect

The first visit is an evaluation: your history, movement tests and a diagnosis from the PT's point of view, followed by a treatment plan that usually includes exercises to do at home.

Credentials to look for

Physical therapists must be licensed by the Minnesota Board of Physical Therapy, and new PTs hold a Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) degree. In Minnesota you can generally see a PT without a referral for up to 90 days of treatment; prevention, wellness and exercise services are not subject to that limit. Check with your insurance plan, which may still require a referral to pay.

Questions to ask before you book

  • Are you licensed in Minnesota?
  • Do you specialize in my kind of problem (for example sports, orthopedics or pelvic health)?
  • How many visits do you expect I will need?
  • Do you take my insurance?

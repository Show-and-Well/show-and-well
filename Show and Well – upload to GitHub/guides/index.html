Find the right kind of wellness professional

Wellness covers more than exercise. These guides explain the seven types of wellness, the professionals who help with each, the credentials to look for in Minnesota and the questions to ask before you book.

Physical wellness

Physical wellness is about how your body moves, recovers, eats and rests.

  • Personal trainers
  • Strength & conditioning coaches
  • Yoga teachers
  • Pilates instructors
  • Massage therapists
  • Physical therapists
  • Dietitians & nutrition coaches

Emotional wellness

Emotional wellness is about understanding and handling your feelings, stress and relationships with yourself.

  • Licensed counselors
  • Therapists
  • Life coaches

Social wellness

Social wellness is about the connections in your life: partners, friends, family and community.

  • Relationship coaches
  • Group fitness instructors
  • Support group facilitators

Spiritual wellness

Spiritual wellness is about meaning, values and a sense of calm.

  • Meditation teachers
  • Breathwork coaches
  • Reiki practitioners

Intellectual wellness

Intellectual wellness is about focus, curiosity and how you learn.

  • Mindfulness teachers
  • Learning & ADHD coaches

Occupational wellness

Occupational wellness is about finding satisfaction and balance in your work.

  • Career coaches
  • Workplace wellness consultants

Environmental wellness

Environmental wellness is about the spaces you live and work in, and your time outdoors.

  • Ergonomics consultants
  • Home organizers
  • Nature & forest therapy guides

Home organizers in Minneapolis–St. Paul

A professional organizer helps you sort, decide what to keep and set up simple systems so your home stays organized. Organizers work without judgment.

See home organizers on Show & WellNot sure? Take the 4-question quiz

What a professional organizer can help with

  • Decluttering a room or a whole home
  • Moving, downsizing or combining households
  • Setting up systems for paperwork, kitchens or closets
  • Chronic disorganization

What to expect

Expect a walk-through or video call to understand your space and goals, then hands-on sessions. You make the decisions about what to keep.

Credentials to look for

Professional organizing is not licensed. Look for membership in NAPO (the National Association of Productivity & Organizing Professionals) or the Certified Professional Organizer (CPO) credential.

Questions to ask before you book

  • How do you work with clients who feel overwhelmed?
  • Do you help with donating or removing items?
  • How do you protect my privacy?

Mindfulness teachers in Minneapolis–St. Paul

A mindfulness teacher helps you practice paying attention to the present moment, on purpose and without judgment. Training often includes short meditations and ways to bring mindfulness into everyday tasks.

See mindfulness teachers on Show & WellNot sure? Take the 4-question quiz

What a mindfulness teacher can help with

  • Stress reduction
  • Focus and attention
  • Handling difficult emotions
  • Building a calmer daily routine

What to expect

Some teachers offer one-on-one sessions; others teach multi-week courses, such as the eight-week Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR) program.

Credentials to look for

Mindfulness teaching is not licensed. Look for teachers trained in an established program, such as MBSR or MBCT teacher training.

Questions to ask before you book

  • What training have you completed?
  • Do you teach one-on-one, courses or both?
  • How much practice time should I plan between sessions?

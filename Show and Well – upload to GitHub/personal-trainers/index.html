Personal trainers in Minneapolis–St. Paul

A personal trainer plans workouts around your goals and current fitness, shows you how to do each exercise safely, and adjusts the plan as you progress. Sessions can be at a gym, in your home, outdoors or online.

What a personal trainer can help with

  • Getting started with exercise, or getting back into it
  • Building strength and muscle
  • Improving cardio fitness and energy
  • Learning proper form so you can train on your own
  • Staying consistent with a routine

What to expect

Most first sessions start with a conversation about your goals, schedule, health history and any injuries, followed by some simple movement tests. Expect to leave with a plan, not just a hard workout.

Credentials to look for

Personal training is not licensed by the state of Minnesota, so certifications matter. Look for a current certification from an NCCA-accredited program, such as NASM, ACE, ACSM or NSCA, plus CPR/AED certification.

Questions to ask before you book

  • What certification do you hold, and is it current?
  • Have you worked with people with goals or limitations like mine?
  • How do you track progress?
  • What is your cancellation policy?

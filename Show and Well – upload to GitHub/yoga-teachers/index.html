Yoga teachers in Minneapolis–St. Paul

A yoga teacher guides you through poses, breathing and relaxation. Private sessions let the teacher adapt every pose to your body, which helps if you are new, stiff, recovering or working toward a specific goal.

What a yoga teacher can help with

  • Flexibility and mobility
  • Posture and body awareness
  • Stress relief and better sleep
  • Building a home practice
  • Gentle movement during recovery or later in life

What to expect

A first private session usually starts with questions about your experience, any injuries and what you want from yoga. Styles range from slow and gentle (restorative, yin) to more active (vinyasa, power).

Credentials to look for

Yoga teaching is not licensed in Minnesota. Many teachers are registered with Yoga Alliance (RYT-200 or RYT-500, based on training hours). Ask about extra training in areas that matter to you, such as prenatal, trauma-informed or therapeutic yoga.

Questions to ask before you book

  • What style do you teach, and is it a good fit for a beginner?
  • How many hours of teacher training have you completed?
  • How do you adapt poses for injuries or limited mobility?

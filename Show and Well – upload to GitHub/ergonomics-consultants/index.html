Ergonomics consultants in Minneapolis–St. Paul

An ergonomics consultant looks at how you sit, stand and work, then recommends changes to your desk, chair, screen and habits to reduce strain.

What a ergonomics consultant can help with

  • Neck, back or wrist strain from desk work
  • Setting up a home office
  • Choosing chairs, desks and equipment
  • Workplace assessments for teams

What to expect

A typical assessment includes watching you work, measuring your setup and giving you a short list of changes. Many consultants also offer virtual assessments by video.

Credentials to look for

Ergonomics is not licensed in Minnesota. Look for a Certified Professional Ergonomist (CPE) or similar certification. Some physical and occupational therapists also offer ergonomic assessments. Ongoing pain should be checked by a health care provider.

Questions to ask before you book

  • What certification do you hold?
  • Do you assess in person or by video?
  • Will I get written recommendations?

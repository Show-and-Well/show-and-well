Physical wellness professionals in Minneapolis–St. Paul

Physical wellness is about how your body moves, recovers, eats and rests. The right professional depends on what you want: building strength, easing pain, recovering from an injury, or changing how you eat.

Browse physical wellness professionalsNot sure? Take the 4-question quiz

Which professional is right for you?

  • Want to get stronger or fitter? Start with a personal trainer or a strength coach.
  • Want to move better and feel less stiff? Yoga and Pilates teachers focus on mobility, posture and control.
  • Recovering from an injury or surgery? A physical therapist is the licensed professional for that.
  • Changing how you eat? A licensed dietitian can give medical nutrition advice; a nutrition coach helps with habits.
  • Want to relax tight muscles? A massage therapist can help with tension and recovery.
